Data Deletion Request

Website: KavyaVibes.com

Last updated: November 10, 2026

At KavyaVibes, we respect your privacy. You can request deletion of personal information associated with your use of our website or community services by following the instructions below.

How to request data deletion

  1. Send an email to our support address.
  2. Use the subject line DATA DELETION REQUEST.
  3. Tell us enough information to help identify the relevant website visit or account, such as the email address you used to contact us or your Telegram username, if applicable.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you are making the request.

Email a Data Deletion Request

What information may be deleted?

Depending on the information we hold and applicable legal requirements, we will review requests to delete information such as support correspondence, website records, visitor or tracking records, and records associated with Telegram join requests.

Verification and processing

We may ask for reasonable information to verify your request and protect other people's information. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, government identity documents, or other sensitive documents by email.

We will review your request and take appropriate action where deletion is possible and legally permitted. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

Third-party services

Some information may be processed or retained independently by third-party services such as Telegram, Meta, Supabase, or Vercel. Deleting information from KavyaVibes does not automatically delete information held by those providers. You may need to contact the relevant provider directly or use its own privacy controls.
