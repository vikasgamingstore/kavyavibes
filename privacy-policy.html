Privacy Policy

Website: KavyaVibes.com

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

KavyaVibes respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you visit our website or use our community features.

2. Information We Collect

  • Browser, device and website usage information.
  • IP address and basic technical information.
  • Advertising attribution information such as fbclid, fbc and fbp, where available.
  • Website visit and interaction information.
  • Telegram join-request information, including identifiers required to associate a join request with a website visit.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate the website, create Telegram invitation links, process join requests, maintain security, respond to support requests and measure advertising performance.

4. Advertising and Conversion Measurement

We may use Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API to measure advertising performance. Depending on your browser settings and available information, advertising identifiers and website interaction data may be processed for attribution and measurement.

Telegram identifiers may be transformed using hashing before being used as external identifiers for conversion measurement. Hashing is a security measure, but it does not necessarily make information anonymous.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services, including:

  • Telegram for community access and invitation links.
  • Meta for advertising and conversion measurement.
  • Supabase for database and image storage.
  • Vercel for website hosting.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the website, provide requested features, measure advertising or comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information. See our Data Deletion page for instructions.

10. Children's Privacy

Our community is intended for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.
